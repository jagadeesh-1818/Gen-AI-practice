# NexaTech Solutions – Employee Handbook 2026

## 1. Company Overview

NexaTech Solutions is a Bengaluru-based technology company established in 2018. The company specializes in Artificial Intelligence, Cloud Computing, Data Engineering, and Enterprise Software Development.

The company currently employs 450 people across three offices: Bengaluru, Hyderabad, and Pune.

Its mission is to help businesses automate repetitive operations using intelligent software systems.

The company operates five major departments:
- Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning
- Software Engineering
- Data Science and Analytics
- Human Resources
- Business Development

## 2. Working Hours and Attendance Policy

The standard working schedule is Monday to Friday, from 9:30 AM to 6:30 PM.

Employees must complete eight working hours daily, excluding a one-hour lunch break.

The company follows a hybrid working model. Employees are required to work from the office at least three days per week. The remaining two days can be worked remotely with manager approval.

Employees arriving more than 15 minutes late must record the reason in the attendance management system.

Three consecutive unapproved absences may result in disciplinary action.

Employees working on weekends to meet project deadlines are eligible for compensatory leave, provided their manager approves the request in advance.

## 3. Leave Policy

Full-time employees receive the following annual leave allowances:

- Casual Leave: 12 days
- Sick Leave: 10 days
- Earned Leave: 15 days
- Public Holidays: As per the official company holiday calendar.

Employees cannot carry forward unused casual leave into the following year.

Unused earned leave can be carried forward up to a maximum of 30 days.

Sick leave exceeding two consecutive working days requires a medical certificate.

Employees must submit casual leave requests at least two working days in advance. Emergency leave can be requested on the same day.

Interns receive one casual leave day for every completed month of their internship. Interns are not eligible for earned leave.

## 4. Internship Program

NexaTech offers internships lasting between three and six months.

The internship program is designed for graduates and final-year students interested in software development, AI, machine learning, and data analytics.

Monthly stipends are determined by the department:

- AI/ML Intern: ₹25,000
- Software Engineering Intern: ₹22,000
- Data Analytics Intern: ₹20,000
- Business Development Intern: ₹15,000

Interns working on exceptional projects may receive a performance bonus of up to ₹10,000 per quarter.

Interns are evaluated every 30 days. Their evaluation considers technical ability, communication, teamwork, attendance, and project contributions.

Interns who achieve a performance score of 85% or higher may be considered for a full-time position. However, conversion is not guaranteed and depends on available vacancies and final management approval.

## 5. Salary and Performance Reviews

Full-time employees receive their salaries on the last working day of every month.

The salary structure consists of:
- Basic Salary
- House Rent Allowance
- Special Allowance
- Performance Bonus, where applicable

Salary revisions generally occur once every year during April.

Employees must complete at least six months of service to qualify for their first annual performance review.

Performance ratings are classified as follows:

- 90–100: Outstanding
- 75–89: Exceeds Expectations
- 60–74: Meets Expectations
- 40–59: Needs Improvement
- Below 40: Unsatisfactory

An employee receiving an Outstanding rating may be eligible for a salary increase of 12–18%.

An employee receiving a Needs Improvement rating must undergo a 60-day performance improvement plan.

Salary increases are not automatic and depend on company performance, individual contributions, and management approval.

## 6. Remote Work Policy

Employees can request permanent remote work after completing one year of service.

Permanent remote work requires approval from both the department head and HR.

Employees working remotely must:
- Maintain availability between 10 AM and 5 PM.
- Attend scheduled virtual meetings.
- Use company-approved devices for confidential work.
- Follow the same information security policies as office employees.

Remote employees are responsible for maintaining a reliable internet connection.

The company provides a monthly internet reimbursement of ₹1,000 to employees approved for permanent remote work.

This reimbursement is not available to employees following the regular hybrid schedule.

## 7. Employee Benefits

Full-time employees receive the following benefits:

1. Medical insurance coverage of ₹5 lakh per year.
2. Accidental insurance coverage of ₹10 lakh.
3. Free access to selected online learning platforms.
4. Annual team-building activities.
5. Reimbursement of up to ₹15,000 per year for approved professional certifications.

Certification reimbursement requires prior manager approval and proof of successful completion.

Interns receive access to learning platforms but are not eligible for company-sponsored medical insurance or certification reimbursement.

## 8. Resignation and Notice Period

The standard notice period for full-time employees is 60 calendar days.

Employees with less than one year of service may request a reduced notice period of 30 days.

Notice period reductions require HR and department head approval.

Employees must return company equipment, including laptops, access cards, and other assigned assets, before receiving their final settlement.

Final settlement is generally processed within 30 working days after the employee's last working day.

Employees who leave without completing their contractual notice period may be subject to applicable contractual recovery provisions.

## 9. Information Security Policy

Employees must never share company credentials, confidential documents, or customer information with unauthorized individuals.

Company data must only be stored using approved cloud storage platforms.

Employees must enable multi-factor authentication on all company accounts.

Personal USB storage devices are prohibited on company-issued computers unless explicitly authorized by the IT department.

Any suspected data breach must be reported to the IT security team within two hours of discovery.

Employees violating information security policies may face disciplinary action depending on the severity of the incident.

## 10. Learning and Development

NexaTech encourages employees to continuously improve their technical skills.

The company provides an annual learning budget of ₹20,000 to full-time employees who have completed at least six months of service.

This budget can be used for:
- Technical certifications
- Professional training programs
- Approved online courses
- Technical conferences

The learning budget cannot be converted into cash or transferred to another employee.

Employees must submit reimbursement requests within 30 days of completing the course.

## 11. Employee Referral Program

Employees can refer candidates for open positions through the internal recruitment portal.

Referral bonuses are determined by the position:

- Software Engineer: ₹10,000
- AI/ML Engineer: ₹20,000
- Data Scientist: ₹15,000
- Engineering Manager: ₹30,000

The referral bonus is paid only after the referred candidate completes three months of employment.

Employees cannot receive referral bonuses for candidates they directly supervise or for their own applications.

## 12. Policy Updates

The HR department reviews company policies every January.

Policy changes are communicated through official company email.

The 2026 handbook became effective on January 1, 2026.

In cases where an individual employment contract contains provisions that differ from this handbook, the applicable signed employment contract takes precedence, subject to applicable law.
